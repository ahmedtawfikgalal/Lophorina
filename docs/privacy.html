Lophorina — Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026

Lophorina is a Chrome extension that lets you control YouTube videos with hand gestures. This policy explains what the extension does with your data. In short: video from your camera is processed on your device and never leaves it, and Lophorina collects nothing about you.

Camera

  • The camera is used only after you press Start Control, and stops when you press Stop Control or close the Lophorina panel.
  • Camera frames are analysed on your device by an on-device hand-gesture model that is bundled with the extension.
  • Video is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere: not to us and not to any third party.

Websites

  • Lophorina can only interact with pages on youtube.com. On a YouTube video page it plays, pauses, or skips the video when you make a gesture or press a button in the panel.
  • It reads the title of the active YouTube tab only to show which video you are controlling. This stays on your device.
  • It does not read your browsing history, cookies, accounts, comments, or any other page content.

Data stored on your device

  • Your gesture settings (which gestures are on, and the hold time) are saved in Chrome’s local extension storage. They never leave your computer and are removed when you uninstall the extension.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • No data is sold, shared, or transferred to anyone.
  • No remote code: everything the extension runs is included in the package reviewed by the Chrome Web Store. The gesture library’s built-in usage reporting is blocked by the extension’s security policy, so it cannot send anything either.
